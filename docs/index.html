Lubanja

Bulk upload TikTok content with AI-powered captions — review, approve, and send drafts from your browser.

About

Lubanja is a web tool for content creators and businesses who manage multiple TikTok posts. Upload images and videos, generate sales-optimized captions with AI, and send everything to your TikTok inbox as drafts — all in batches.

Key Features

Batch Upload

Upload multiple videos and images at once. Review them side-by-side before sending to TikTok.

AI Captions

Automatically generate captions and hashtags using Google Gemini. Optimized for sales and TikTok SEO.

Visual Recognition

Gemini analyses each product image to write accurate, specific descriptions.

Draft to Inbox

Uploaded content lands in your TikTok inbox as drafts — review and publish from the TikTok app.
